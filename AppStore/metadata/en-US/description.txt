Can I burn wood today? Stookwijzer shows the wood-burning advice from the Dutch RIVM (National Institute for Public Health and the Environment) for your location: code yellow, orange or red. You'll know when it's better to leave your wood stove, fireplace or fire pit unlit and avoid bothering your neighbours.

ADVICE FOR YOUR PLACE
• The current advice for your location or any place you choose
• A clear explanation of every code: what it means and why it applies now
• Wind (Beaufort and m/s) and air quality (LKI index) at a glance
• See right away when the code will change

FORECAST
• Advice in 6-hour blocks for the coming days
• Spot final and provisional blocks at a glance

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
• A heads-up as soon as the code for one of your places changes
• Your choice: on code orange and red, or code red only
• Optionally a notification when it's back to code yellow
• Code red arrives as a time-sensitive notification

WIDGETS
• Small and medium widgets for your Home Screen
• Lock Screen widgets
• The colour updates automatically with the forecast

MULTIPLE PLACES
• Save home, your holiday cottage or family
• Search by town, postcode or address

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
• On iPad you see all your places in the sidebar with the advice beside it
• Light and dark mode, no account needed, no ads

PLEASE NOTE
The app is in Dutch and the advice covers the Netherlands only.

PRIVACY
Your location is only used for the advice. If you turn on notifications, your places are sent to our notification server rounded to about 1 kilometre. We collect nothing else and don't track you.

ABOUT THE DATA
The advice comes from the open data of the RIVM Stookwijzer (Atlas Leefomgeving). This app is not made by RIVM or the Dutch government. Local rules on wood burning differ per municipality.
