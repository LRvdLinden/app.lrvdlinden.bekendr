Want to know what is about to change in your neighbourhood? Bekendr shows the official public notices (bekendmakingen) from Dutch municipalities, provinces and water boards that concern your area. A dormer at the neighbours, a street that will be closed, trees being felled or a new zoning plan: you see it right away, in one place.

Choose an address with a radius, or a neighbourhood, district or entire municipality. Bekendr fetches the notices directly from officielebekendmakingen.nl and keeps track of them for you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Notices around your address (radius from 100 m to 2 km), or in a neighbourhood, district or municipality
• Combine several neighbourhoods or districts into one location
• Follow several locations, such as home, work or your parents
• Clear categories: permits, traffic, trees and felling, events, zoning plans and other rules
• Choose per location which categories you want to see
• All notices as a list or on the map, with a colour per category
• For each notice the distance, the authority, a summary and a link to the official publication
• Save, mark as read or swipe to the trash
• A notification when something new appears, with quiet hours at night
• Home Screen widget
• Share an address from another app to add it as a location
• Lock with Face ID or Touch ID
• Your locations and choices in sync via iCloud on iPhone, iPad and Mac

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Bekendr has no server of its own: the app fetches the data itself from the public government sources (KOOP and PDOK). Your locations stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

GOOD TO KNOW
Bekendr covers the Netherlands only and the app is available in Dutch. Bekendr is not a government app.
